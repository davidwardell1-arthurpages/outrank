How it works

Every question has exactly five options and exactly one right answer: the other four sit clearly on the wrong side of your card. Near-ties are never asked, so small differences between data sources can't trip you up. Easy keeps the gaps wide and the countries familiar and shows your card's value. Hard narrows the gaps, adds less obvious countries and hides your card's number. Keys 1–5 answer, Enter deals the next card.

The Daily deal is the same chain for everyone on the same day (medium difficulty, one go), so you can compare streaks with friends.
